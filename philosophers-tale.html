Multi-Criteria Hut Site Suitability Analysis

This project investigated potential locations for a new wilderness hut along the Philosopher's Tale Track in Tasmania, considering competing priorities of accessibility, construction costs and landscape characteristics.

Philosopher's Tale Track, Tasmania · 2026
01 / Approach

One landscape, two priorities.

Using GIS-based suitability analysis, I evaluated potential hut locations against environmental and infrastructure constraints, including terrain, proximity to existing tracks and roads, water features and threatened vegetation.

Two different perspectives were explored: an access-focused approach prioritising convenient locations, and a cost-focused approach considering the practical implications of site selection.

Additional viewshed analysis was used to investigate landscape visibility from selected potential hut sites.

04 / Findings

What the comparison showed.

The two suitability approaches identified different preferred hut sites, demonstrating how the weighting of decision-making criteria can influence spatial planning outcomes.

The access-focused approach identified a preferred site of approximately 7,500 m², ranked first for accessibility but 25th for cost suitability.

In comparison, the cost-focused approach identified a preferred site of approximately 1,025 m², ranked first for cost suitability and second for accessibility. This site also had an approximate mapped viewshed area of 24.41 km².

The comparison highlights the importance of considering competing priorities when selecting locations for infrastructure in environmentally sensitive landscapes.
